# Visual asset inventory

- Movie posters and backdrops are loaded from TMDB; their attribution and dataset provenance are described in the backend's DATA_NOTICE.md. They are not relicensed under the application's MIT license.
- The application uses Flutter Material icons for avatars, onboarding and collection placeholders; Flutter's bundled notices are available through the framework license registry.
- Navigation SVGs and the CineMate wordmark are simple project-authored vectors in this release and are covered by the project MIT license.
- The earlier raster placeholders had no recorded source. They are excluded from the application bundle and removed from the current working version instead of asserting an unverified license.
- Screenshots in docs/screenshots are captured from the actual application with synthetic demo accounts. They include TMDB movie imagery; the same attribution applies.
